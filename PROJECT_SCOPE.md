2. How It Works (`/how-it-works`)

4. Rates & Calculator (`/rates-calculator`)

## Non-negotiable project rules

- The approved Canva screenshots are the final visual source of truth.
- Do not use an older KargoDoor website as a design source.
- Do not access, modify, delete, or overwrite older KargoDoor projects.
- Do not deploy or publish to Netlify.
- Do not begin visual implementation until the approved screenshots are supplied.
